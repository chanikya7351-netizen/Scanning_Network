# Lab 04: Scan Beyond IDS and Firewall

## Objective
Understand how filtering, packet manipulation and proxy/VPN routing affect network visibility in a controlled lab.

## Sub-Labs
- [4.1 Evasion Techniques with Nmap](4.1-Nmap-Evasion/README.md)
- [4.3 Custom UDP/TCP Packets with Hping3](4.3-Hping3-Custom-Packets/README.md)
- [4.4 Proxy Switcher](4.4-Proxy-Switcher/README.md)
- [4.5 CyberGhost VPN](4.5-CyberGhost-VPN/README.md)

## Safety
These exercises are for isolated, authorized workshop networks. Do not use evasion techniques against systems you do not own or have explicit permission to test.

## Key idea
Evasion does not mean "bypassing every firewall." It means changing scan characteristics in ways that may affect detection, filtering or packet handling. Modern IDS/IPS and firewalls can normalize or detect many such techniques.
