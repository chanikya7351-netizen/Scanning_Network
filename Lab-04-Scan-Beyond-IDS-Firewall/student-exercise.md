# Lab 04 Student Exercise

## Part A: Nmap
Compare a baseline SYN scan with one packet-modification option. Capture both in Wireshark.


## Part B: Hping3
Send TCP SYN and UDP packets to a controlled test service.

## Part C: Proxy
Capture traffic with the proxy disabled and enabled.

## Part D: VPN
Record the public IP before and after connecting to the VPN.

### Questions
1. What changes when packets are fragmented?
2. Why does a TCP scan need return traffic?
3. Why might a custom packet produce no SYN-ACK?
4. How does a proxy differ from a VPN?
5. Why is "anonymous" not the same as "untraceable"?
